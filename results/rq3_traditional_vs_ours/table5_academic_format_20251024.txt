The results demonstrate that incorporating semantic embeddings (Word2Vec +
DistilBERT) through attention-based fusion produces significantly more
semantically coherent topic clusters compared to purely structure-based

objective), our approach prioritizes semantic coherence while additionally
providing rich embedding representations suitable for downstream tasks.

The large effect sizes (Cohen's d = 0.96 and 1.68) indicate not only
statistical significance but also substantial practical importance of the
observed improvements in semantic coherence.
